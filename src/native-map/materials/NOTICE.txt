Noitamap native material resolver.

The C algorithms and generated immutable tables are derived from Lymm's
Telescope source, including the pinned vitaminmoo render-perf fork contained in
lib/noita-telescope-vm. Upstream MIT attribution is reproduced in
LICENSE-telescope.txt and must accompany any promoted source or binary.

Copyright (c) 2026 Lymm

Source algorithms: js/engine_resolve/topo2_resolve.js, perlin_noise.js,
carve_noise.js, simplex_noise.js, band_select.js, chunk_wobble.js and
material_field.js and lattice_builder.js. Vertical plane address translation
follows src/telescope/plane-material-field.ts (also pinned in the manifest).
Source tables: js/engine_resolve/engine_data.js.
Exact source and compiled artifact SHA256 hashes are retained in
build-identity.json. Vite validates them before building or starting development.

The WASM core, band and ownership ports preserve the selected JavaScript
reference's numeric semantics. This establishes source parity only; inherited
Telescope inaccuracies are not independently proven correct against Noita.
No game executable or scene artwork is bundled in these WASM artifacts.

Compiler: WASI SDK 34, Clang, --target=wasm32-unknown-unknown, -O3,
-ffp-contract=off -fno-fast-math. No relaxed SIMD or fast-math is used.
Build with an external installed SDK:
  WASI_SDK_PATH=/path/to/wasi-sdk node build_scripts/build-native-materials.mjs
Check without a compiler:
  node build_scripts/build-native-materials.mjs --check
No compiler is downloaded by the application. The SDK's distribution carries
its own toolchain licenses.
